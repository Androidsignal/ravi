## Portfolio Images — Drag & Drop

The site accepts **drag-and-drop images** anywhere a placeholder appears — no code edits needed. Drop an image onto a slot and it stays put (the choice persists across reloads).

Slots that accept a dropped image:

- **Hero portrait** — Ravi's headshot / profile photo (top of the page).
- **Project thumbnails (×19)** — one per card in the work grid; also reorderable by drag.
- **Case-study hero** — the large image at the top of each `work/*.html` detail page.
- **Case-study gallery** — screenshot slots inside each project's body (device-framed).

**Guidance for dropped images**

- Thumbnails read best as **4:3 or 16:10** app screenshots or device mockups on a flat/neutral background.
- Case-study heroes: **wide (16:9)** — a single strong screen or a device composition.
- Keep to real product screenshots or device frames — no stock photography, per the design system.
- Prefer **PNG/WebP**, ~1600px on the long edge; images are lazy-loaded, so larger files are fine.

_Drag-and-drop is the intended way to add real imagery — replace every placeholder before sharing the site._
