KEY FEATURES
01On-Demand Creator Access

Reach doctors, lawyers, instructors and creators on demand via text, voice and video.

02Monetised Interactions

Verified sellers set their own per-message or per-minute rate; users pay only when interacting — no subscription.

03Privacy-First & Data-Efficient

Username-based, not phone numbers; all communication runs over data, avoiding SMS/MMS fees.

04Real-Time Translation

Messages translate live across 15 languages; add contacts by username, QR code or social link.

05WebRTC Voice & Video

Low-latency, encrypted peer-to-peer calling with adaptive bitrate across Android and iOS.

06Creator Ecosystem

Account verification, custom pricing, a personal ultra.bio link and monthly deposits across 120+ countries.

CHALLENGES & SOLUTIONS

A globally scalable video-calling and messaging platform meant mastering peer-to-peer networking, encryption and instant delivery.

01

Global WebRTC Scaling

Connecting users across continents caused high latency and dropped packets.

Solution

Deployed a distributed network of TURN servers worldwide for the shortest relay path when P2P fails.

02

End-to-End Encryption

Absolute privacy for messages and video calls was paramount.

Solution

Integrated the Signal Protocol into Flutter so encryption keys never leave the user's device.

03

Background Call Wakeup

Incoming calls failed to wake Android devices due to OS restrictions.

Solution

Used high-priority VoIP push (APNs/FCM) wired directly to native CallKit/ConnectionService.

04

Media Compression

Sending 4K videos used too much data and took too long.

Solution

Built a multi-threaded background engine that transcodes video to H.264 locally before upload.
