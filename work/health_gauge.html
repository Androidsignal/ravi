KEY FEATURES
01Real-Time Health Monitoring

Track heart rate, blood pressure, activity and body weight in real time, synced accurately from connected devices.

02Wearable & Smart Device Integration

Bluetooth health devices and smart weight scales pair seamlessly for continuous data collection.

03Interactive Graphs & Animations

Charts, animations and progress graphs turn complex health data into clear, actionable insight.

04FCM Notifications & Alerts

Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers instant health alerts, reminders and scheduled notifications.

05HTTP API Integration

Secure HTTP APIs connect to third-party services, wearable platforms and external health systems.

06Scheduled Health Reminders

Automated reminders keep routines on track — medication, activity goals and regular checks.

CHALLENGES & SOLUTIONS

A sophisticated health-tracking platform meant integrating complex Bluetooth LE wearables and rendering massive health-metric datasets.

01

Bluetooth LE Stability

Holding a constant connection to health wearables without killing the phone battery.

Solution

Native background BLE services buffer data on the wearable and bulk-sync only when optimal.

02

Heavy Data Visualization

Heart-rate charts with over 10,000 data points froze the app.

Solution

Custom Canvas painting in Flutter dynamically downsamples by zoom level to hold 60fps.

03

Cross-Device Data Sync

Accessing data across multiple devices caused synchronization conflicts.

Solution

Built a CRDT (conflict-free replicated data type) architecture guaranteeing integrity across platforms.

04

Secure Health Alerts

Real-time health notifications had to meet strict medical regulations.

Solution

Enforced end-to-end encryption on push payloads, with biometric processing on-device via Firebase Cloud Functions.
