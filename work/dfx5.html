CHALLENGES & SOLUTIONS

Building an AI Agent Contact Center meant mastering real-time audio streaming, speech-to-text and low-latency VoIP architecture.

01

Live Speech Transcription

Transcribing calls in real time introduced unacceptable latency into the conversation.

Solution

Streamed audio chunks over direct WebSockets to specialized speech-to-text models for sub-second transcription.

02

VoIP Call Stability

Maintaining high-quality audio over fluctuating mobile networks was difficult.

Solution

Implemented WebRTC with adaptive bitrates, seamlessly downgrading quality to prevent dropped calls.

03

AI Sentiment Analysis

Running sentiment analysis on live text froze the UI.

Solution

Offloaded all NLP to background isolates in Flutter, keeping the main UI thread completely unblocked.

04

Complex Call Routing

Managing agent availability and call queues in real time demanded absolute precision.

Solution

Built a scalable backend managing stateful WebSocket connections for thousands of concurrent agents.
