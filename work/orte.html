CASE FILE · 10 / 19 FITNESS

Orte

Fitness & health insights with personalized analytics and readiness tracking.

FlutterFirebaseHealthKitGoogle Fit
MY ROLE

Built the Flutter app end to end — health-data sync, the readiness scoring UI, and the daily challenge engine.

4.8App Store rating
60fpsChart rendering
iOS + AndroidSingle codebase
01

The problem

Users had health data scattered across wearables and native health stores, with no single place that turned it into something motivating and actionable.

02

The approach

I designed a clean Flutter architecture around a unified health-data layer, pulling from HealthKit and Google Fit and normalizing it into a readiness score. The UI leans on smooth, native-feeling charts and a daily challenge loop to keep people coming back.

03

The result

A cross-platform app that reads like a native one on both stores, with real-time analytics that hold 60fps even on mid-range devices.

KEY FEATURES
01Readiness & Health Scoring

Analyzes heart rate variability, resting heart rate and respiratory data into a clear daily readiness score.

02Personalized Fitness Insights

Actionable insights from activity patterns, performance trends and recovery levels for smarter training.

03Challenges & Gamification

Fitness challenges, streaks and reward points that drive long-term engagement and healthy habits.

04Advanced Analytics Dashboard

Interactive charts tracking strength, cardio and wellness progress over time.

05Integrated Fitness Marketplace

A built-in marketplace for premium equipment, wearables and training solutions, purchasable in-app.

06Cross-Platform Flutter Architecture

Consistent UI, high performance and scalability across both Android and iOS.

CHALLENGES & SOLUTIONS

Building a modern health, fitness and lifestyle intelligence app meant fusing real-time analytics, gamified challenges and an e-commerce marketplace into one fluid experience.

01

Real-Time Fitness Analytics

Processing and displaying large volumes of performance and readiness data caused UI lag.

Solution

Offloaded complex data aggregation to native background isolates, keeping the Flutter UI thread perfectly fluid.

02

Integrated Fitness Marketplace

Building a secure, smooth e-commerce flow directly within the lifestyle app.

Solution

Engineered a native checkout integrating Stripe APIs for one-click purchases and secure tokenization.

03

Personalized Challenges Engine

Tracking gamified streaks reliably across user timezones broke motivation.

Solution

Built a timezone-agnostic date-calculation engine guaranteeing absolute streak accuracy regardless of travel.

04

Cross-Platform Data Sync

Keeping lifestyle intelligence data perfectly synchronized across platforms.

Solution

Adopted the BLoC pattern with Firebase real-time listeners for instant cross-device consistency.
